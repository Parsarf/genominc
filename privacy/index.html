Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

This page explains what personal information the Genominc website handles and what happens to it. It describes how the site works today.

Who we are

Genominc is a healthcare distributor at P.O. Box 326486, RAKFTZ, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. You can reach us at info@genominc.com or +971 7 207 8763.

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, we receive what you type into it:

  • your name and email address (required)
  • your organisation and phone number (optional)
  • the product area you are asking about (optional)
  • your message

If you email or phone us directly, we receive whatever you choose to tell us.

How the enquiry form works

When you press “Send enquiry”, your browser sends the form to this website’s server, which passes it on as an email to Genominc using the email delivery service Resend. The website does not keep its own database of enquiries. The email is then held in Genominc’s mailbox, and the delivery service and the website host keep their own technical logs of the transmission.

If the form cannot send your enquiry, the page tells you so. In that case Genominc has not received it.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and to deal with any quotation or order that follows from it.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from the website itself; it does not load third-party scripts, fonts or maps. The site is delivered by a hosting provider (currently Vercel), which processes technical request data such as IP address and browser details in order to serve pages.

Questions

To ask about the information you have sent us, email info@genominc.com.

Changes

If the way the site handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
