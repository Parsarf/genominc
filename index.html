About Genominc

A distributor that answers the phone.

Genominc is a healthcare product distributor based in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We supply clinics, laboratories, pharmacies and veterinary practices.

Distribution is a service business before it is a product business. Stock runs out, shipments get held, a clinic needs an item this week rather than next month. We built the company around being reachable and giving straight answers about lead times.

  • Straight answers on lead timesWe tell you what we expect, and tell you when it changes.
  • Orders of different sizesSingle clinics through to group accounts.
  • Paperwork handled in-houseRegistration and import documentation for the products you order.
How it works

From enquiry to delivery in four steps.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send a product list, a catalogue reference, or describe the requirement in your own words.

  2. Quotation and availability

    Pricing, current stock position and an estimated lead time for the items you asked about.

  3. Documents and clearance

    We confirm which registration and import documents the products need and arrange them with you.

  4. Delivery and reorder

    Goods are delivered to your facility. Items you order regularly can be repeated without starting again.
