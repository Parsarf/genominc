About Genominc

A distributor that answers the phone.

Genominc is a healthcare product distribution company based in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We supply clinics, laboratories, pharmacies and veterinary practices with products sourced from manufacturers we work with directly.

Distribution is a service business before it is a product business. Stock runs out, shipments get held, a clinic needs an item this week rather than next month. We built the company around being reachable and giving straight answers about lead times.

Direct sourcingRelationships with manufacturers, not resellers.
Documentation handledRegistration and import paperwork in-house.

How we work

From enquiry to delivery.

Most orders follow the same four steps. You will know where yours stands at each one.

1Tell us what you need

Send a product list, a catalogue reference, or just describe the requirement. We will identify the options.

2Quote and availability

Pricing, current stock position and a realistic lead time, usually within one working day.

3Documentation and clearance

We handle registration checks, import documents and customs so nothing stalls at the border.

4Delivery and reorder

Goods arrive at your facility. Recurring items can be scheduled so you are not chasing stock.
